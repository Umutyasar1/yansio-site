Privacy Policy

Effective: 26 October 2026

YansioTürkçe

Summary

Yansio requires no account, contains no ads or analytics/tracking SDKs, and does not collect your personal data or send it to any server. Your favourites, watch history, groups and settings stay on your device.

Stored on your device

  • Favourites, recents, watch history (90 days), channel groups, recent searches, settings.
  • M3U playlist URLs you add: encrypted with iOS Keychain / Android Keystore.
  • Diagnostics log and "Channel won't open" reports: on device only, passwords in URLs are masked. They go nowhere unless you copy and share them.

Deleting the app or Settings → Privacy → "Delete all data" removes them.

Connections to third parties

To work, the app connects directly to the services below; they can see your IP address and device information (e.g. player user agent) and their own policies apply:

ServicePurpose
Broadcasters' servers and CDNsPlaying the channel/radio you choose
M3U servers you addDownloading and playing your playlist
GitHub (gist.githubusercontent.com)Downloading the signed channel catalogue (no personal data sent)
TVmazeProgramme guide (Premium)

Podcast publishers' serversPodcast feeds and episodes you open
YouTube (Google)Embedded player for channels that stream on YouTube
Apple / Google speech recognitionOnly when you tap voice search; your words are turned into text, Yansio records no audio
App Store / Google PlayPremium purchase and restore (payment details never reach Yansio)

Local network

To send to a TV and mirror the screen, Yansio looks for Chromecast, AirPlay and DLNA devices on your Wi-Fi. This information never leaves your device.

Permissions

Microphone and speech recognition are used only for voice search, local network only for device discovery, notifications only for background audio/mirroring. You can revoke them in system settings at any time.

Children

Yansio is not directed at children under 13. Parents can lock categories with a PIN.

GDPR / KVKK

As we process no personal data on servers, there is nothing for us to delete or export. Questions: destek@yansio.app. Controller: the Yansio developer, Türkiye.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date are updated.
